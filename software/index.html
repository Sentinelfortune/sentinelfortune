Sentinel Fortune publishes two different kinds of thing, and it is worth being clear about which is which. The Digital Shop sells downloadable products — a file you buy once, licensed to you, delivered as a private download. This page is about hosted software: applications that run in your browser, with nothing to download and their own commercial terms.

Floor

Know the price below which a job loses you money.

Your rent, insurance, admin, software and van are paid whether or not you win the job. A quote that covers labour and materials can still lose money, because none of those fixed costs got recovered. That gap is invisible until the year ends.

Floor closes it. You enter four figures once — annual overhead, billable hours per year, what an hour of labour costs you, and the margin you want. It works out what every billable hour has to contribute before anything is profit, then applies that to each job you add.

Who it is for

Owner-operators of small trades and home-service businesses — plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and the trades that quote job by job. It is most useful if you price from experience rather than from a costing model, and have never been certain the experience is right.

What it gives you

What Floor calculates for each job
OutputWhat it means
Overhead recovery rateWhat every billable hour must contribute toward fixed costs. Computed once, applied to every job.
True costLabour, materials, travel and subcontractor cost, plus the overhead those hours are responsible for carrying.
Price floorBreak-even. Below this the job consumes the business rather than funding it.
Target priceWhat the job needed to be priced at to hit your margin — solved properly, not cost plus a percentage.
Yield per hourWhat an hour of that job actually returned after every cost.
DiagnosisWith three or more jobs, which cost component was out of line with your own typical job.

How it works

Enter your figures, add jobs, read the result. The report recalculates as you go, and exports to CSV for your spreadsheet or to a printable sheet you can take to a pricing conversation. A worked example loads with one click, so you can see the output before entering anything of your own.

What it does not do

  • It is not accounting, tax or financial advice — it is arithmetic on your figures.
  • It does not connect to accounting software, a bank, or an invoicing system.
  • It does not sync between devices, which is the same reason nothing is uploaded.
  • It does not require, or offer, an account.

Privacy

Floor has no server component. There is no database, no account system, no analytics, and the application makes no network requests at all — your pricing is commercially sensitive and there is no reason for it to leave your device. The trade-off is stated plainly in the app: clearing your browser data removes your figures, and export is a first-class action rather than an afterthought.

Status and price

Floor is version 1.0 and free to use. It has no server, so it has no running cost per user, and charging before there is evidence people return to it would add billing machinery ahead of demand. If a price is introduced it will be stated here first.
