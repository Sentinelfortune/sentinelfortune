Sentinel Fortune publishes two different kinds of thing, and it is worth being clear about which is which. The Digital Shop sells downloadable products — a file you buy once, licensed to you, delivered as a private download. This page is about hosted software: applications that run in your browser, with nothing to download and their own commercial terms.

Prism

One goal in. One plan out.

You start by describing what you are trying to accomplish, in your own words. Prism works out which of seven dimensions your goal actually needs, opens a focused workspace for each of them, and hands back a single plan you can print or export.

The seven dimensions are drawn from how Sentinel Fortune itself works, and each does one job:

The seven dimensions inside Prism and the job each one does
DimensionWhat it does
Sentinel Fortune RecordsTurns a finished or planned work into a dated release sequence.
LumenGameDesigns a challenge, so engagement is built rather than improvised.
VibraFlow MediaA structured reflection on the questions underneath the work. General and educational.
CodexWorld TVGives a message a shape someone can follow — setup, turn, landing.
Lumen School AcademyTurns an intention into a learning sequence with a first action. Educational only.
LightNode SystemsTurns something done from memory into a written, repeatable workflow.
OG Legacy StoreShapes work into an offer someone could actually buy.

Most goals need two or three of them, not seven. Deciding which is the part Prism does for you — and you can override it.

Who it is for

People who know what they want to build and lose time deciding where to start: operators turning experience into something sellable, creators with a body of work and no release plan, anyone holding a goal that spans making something, explaining it, delivering it and pricing it.

What it does not do

  • It does not do the work. It produces a plan; you execute it.
  • It does not give professional advice of any kind — legal, financial, tax, investment, medical or mental-health.
  • It does not sync between devices. Projects live in the browser you created them in, which is the same reason nothing you write is ever uploaded.
  • It does not require, or offer, an account.

Privacy

Prism has no server component. There is no database, no account system and no analytics — everything you enter is held in your own browser's local storage and never leaves the device. The reflection dimension in particular is treated as personal content: it is stored the same way as everything else, which is to say locally, and it appears in an export only if you generate one.

The trade-off is worth stating plainly: clearing your browser data will remove your projects, and they will not follow you to another device. Export is the answer to both, and it is a first-class action rather than an afterthought.

Status

Prism is an early version. It is free, it is complete enough to produce a real plan end to end, and it is published here rather than in the Shop because it is software you use, not a file you buy. It is not yet available at a public address — this page will carry the link when it is.
