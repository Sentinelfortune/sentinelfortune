The House

Seven houses, one company

Sentinel Fortune LLC operates under one master domain. Each identity is a house within it — a distinct problem domain with its own way of working — not a separate business. Each will open at its own address when it is ready. None of them has opened yet.

Every house below is in build. Each one is listed with the customer problems it owns, so it is clear what it is for before there is much in it. Work belongs to a house because of the problem it solves, never because of the format it ships in.
