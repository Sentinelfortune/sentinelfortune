This catalogue is dedicated to Sentinel Fortune's downloadable digital products. Everything in it is a finished digital file — not a course platform, not a membership, not software you have to keep paying for. You buy it, you download it, and it is yours to use under the licence attached to it.

Sentinel Fortune also creates other classes of product, including hosted software. Those are not sold here: when one is available it is presented on its own surface, with the commercial terms that actually apply to it.

What is not included with a downloadable product

  • Professional advice of any kind — legal, financial, tax, HR, insurance, safety, regulatory, code, permit or licensing.
  • Hosting, or any ongoing service — a downloadable product is a file, not a subscription. Sentinel Fortune's hosted software, where it exists, is a separate offering.
  • Done-for-you implementation. These are materials you apply to your own business.

If you need something adapted to your organisation rather than bought off the shelf, get in touch and describe what you need.

Where to start

If you are not sure a product is for you, read first. The Guides cover how to choose and apply this kind of material, the Articles go deeper into the operational thinking behind it, and the Updates are short notes on single ideas. All of it is free and none of it requires an email address.
