Before you buy

Questions people actually ask

Do I need an account to buy?
No. Checkout runs through Stripe and delivery goes to the email address you enter there. Nothing to create, nothing to log into afterwards.
How do I receive what I bought?
A private download link appears on the confirmation page immediately after payment and is emailed to the address used at checkout.
What happens if the download link expires?
Email us and it is reissued. Links are time-limited and download-limited for security, not to cut a purchase short.
Is this a subscription?
No. Every product is a single purchase with no recurring charge and nothing to renew.
Can I use a product across several businesses?
That depends on the licence named on the product page. Single-business is the default; multi-location, consultant and white-label licences exist for some products.
Is any of this professional advice?
No. Everything published is operational and general. It is not legal, financial, tax, HR, insurance, safety, regulatory, code, permit or licensing advice, and it does not replace a professional licensed in your jurisdiction.
