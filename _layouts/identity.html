--- layout: base --- {%- comment -%} The shared shell for the seven identity experiences. WHAT THIS LAYOUT OWNS, AND WHAT IT DELIBERATELY DOES NOT -------------------------------------------------------- It owns the frame: breadcrumb, masthead, honest readiness statement, problem domain, growth surface, address line. Everything that must be identical across the seven so they read as one company. It does NOT own the experience. `{{ content }}` is a hole in the middle of the page, and each identity fills it with its own structure — a transport and a waveform, a two-pane codex, a launch tile, a numbered spine, a single reading column, system cards, a lookbook rail. That is the whole point: seven pages sharing a frame are one house with seven rooms, seven pages sharing an information architecture are one website printed seven times. Front matter each identity page must set: identity the slug, matched against _data/identities.yml stylesheets identity.css plus that identity's own experience stylesheet {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign identity = site.data.identities | where: "slug", page.identity | first -%} {%- include identity-state.html identity=identity -%} {%- comment -%} Copied out under distinct names immediately. The siblings navigation at the bottom calls identity-state.html again, once per sibling, and each call overwrites identity_state / identity_ready / identity_linked / identity_remote in this shared scope. Anything about THIS identity must therefore be read from the `this_*` copies, which nothing else touches. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign this_state = identity_state -%} {%- assign this_ready = identity_ready -%} {%- assign this_remote = identity_remote -%}
