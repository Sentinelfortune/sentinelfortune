What a license gives you

A Single Business license lets you use the purchased product — including adapting and customizing the included materials — internally, within your own business. It does not transfer ownership of the underlying frameworks, methodologies, templates, or brand assets used to build the product.

What a license does not allow

  • Reselling, sublicensing, or redistributing the product as a standalone product
  • Sharing the files outside the licensed business
  • Using the materials to build a directly competing template/toolkit product for resale
